How to Reach Hotel Grand 81

Clear point-to-point driving instructions from major connecting corridors.

From Delhi & Badarpur Border

Take the Delhi-Agra Highway (NH-19). Proceed straight past Neelam Chowk Flyover and Bata Flyover. Take the left exit toward Sector 81 Bypass / BPTP bridge, leading directly onto World Street and Vipul Plaza.

From Delhi Metro (Violet Line)

De-board at Bata Chowk or Escorts Mujesar metro station. Take an auto-rickshaw or taxi directly across the Agra Canal bypass bridge to Vipul Plaza, Sector 81 (approx. 10 mins).

From Gurgaon via Expressway

Take the Faridabad-Gurgaon Expressway to BK Chowk. Follow the BPTP bypass road directly into Sector 81. Our hotel is situated at Vipul Plaza right opposite World Street Mall.
